### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Physical Science Undergraduate** majoring in **ICT, Pure Mathematics, and Physics** at the **University of Sri Jayewardenepura (USJ)**, Sri Lanka. Alongside my academic pursuits, I am an entrepreneurial tech builder serving as the **Founder, LMS Administrator, and Operational Manager at AITS College (Pitakotte)** and the operator of software venture **PTM Prime**.

- 🎓 **Academic Base:** B.Sc. in Physical Science (ICT, Pure Mathematics, Physics) — *University of Sri Jayewardenepura*
- 🏛️ **Leadership & Ops:** Founder & LMS Architect @ *AITS College (Pitakotte)*
- 💡 **Engineering Venture:** Founder & Lead Developer @ *PTM Prime* (Developer Tooling, Automation & Web Apps)
- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Large-scale community platforms, web automation scripts, progressive web applications, and modern cloud architectures
- ⚡ **Core Philosophy:** Clean code, high usability, and building technology that solves real-world challenges for students, businesses, and developers
